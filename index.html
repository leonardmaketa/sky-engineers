About us

Where ecological integrity meets human development

A Tanzanian consultancy serving government agencies, development partners, utilities, private developers and communities.

Sky Engineers Ltd specialises in water resources engineering, hydrology, GIS and environmental management. Our team brings together engineers, hydrologists, climate specialists, GIS analysts and regulatory experts, combining technical depth with field experience on Tanzanian roads, rivers and water supply schemes.

We model catchments and floods with SWAT, HBV, HEC-HMS and HEC-RAS, map risk with satellite data and field surveys, and turn the results into designs, approvals and reports that clients can act on.

We know the Tanzanian approvals landscape first-hand: NEMC environmental procedures, EWURA licensing, basin water boards, TANROADS standards, district councils and the ministries that sign off on projects.

Research

Science behind our hydrology

Our Managing Director's PhD research at the University of Dar es Salaam keeps our methods at the front of the field. The same tools and data serve our clients.

Katuma River – Lake Katavi, western Tanzania

The PhD investigates how surface water and groundwater interact across the 3,656 km² Katuma catchment, which feeds Lake Katavi and Katavi National Park. It builds on an MSc on how climate and land use change have altered streamflow in the same basin.

  • Semi-distributed HBV and SWAT+ models calibrated against river gauges
  • Testing global streamflow products (GeoGLOWS) against local gauge records
  • CHIRPS rainfall climatology and climate downscaling for 1991–2020 and beyond
  • Field surveys of channels, gauging sites and wetlands
  • Environmental flow needs at the Katavi National Park reach

What this means for clients: tested models, honest uncertainty, and hydrology that holds up where gauge data are sparse.

How we work

From kickoff to approval, one accountable team

A clear project management framework keeps every assignment on time, on budget and to standard, with risk and stakeholder management built in from day one.

  1. Inception

    Scope, deliverables and lines of communication agreed with the client at a formal kickoff.

  2. Field investigation
